Amy and I are hiking seven nature preserves as part of Southeast Michigan Land
Conservancy's Walk in Wonder Hike-a-thon. We hope these posts and photos convey
our deep enjoyment and appreciation for these spaces, and inspire you to
discover the nature preserves near you.

1. <a href="/2026-07-11-walk-in-wonder-springhill-preserve">Springhill Preserve</a>

6. <a href="/2026-08-19-walk-in-wonder-sibley-prairie">Sibley Prairie</a>
7. LeFurge Woods (_coming soon_)

SMLC is an incredible organization, preserving land that is critical for bird
migration, and for people like us to find an escape into nature. If you'd like
to sponsor our hike-a-thon, you can do that
[here](https://givebutter.com/WalkinWonder/amyarongriffis).
