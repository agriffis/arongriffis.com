_First of seven hikes in the <a href="/2026-08-29-walk-in-wonder">Walk in Wonder 2026 hike-a-thon</a>._

Over the next few weeks, Amy and I plan to hike seven nature preserves, as part
of Southeast Michigan Land Conservancy's "Walk in Wonder Hike-a-thon." We'll be
sharing photos and info from each hike, and welcome any comments or questions
you might have.

Our first hike is Springhill Preserve in Superior Township. The property is
small (30 acres) but rich in biodiversity. It also abuts Kosch-Headwaters
Preserve (160 acres, owned by the county) so you can easily hike a couple hours
if you combine them. The Springhill trail runs next to a field and then loops
through a mature beech/maple/oak woods. We're always excited to hike this trail
because of the buttonbush next to the pond. That's the spiky white globe in the
photos!

The beautiful blue bird is an Indigo Bunting. (It has an amazing song, but
you'll have to go to hear it!) The butterfly is is an Eyed Brown (I think!) and
the dragonfly is a Green Darner.
