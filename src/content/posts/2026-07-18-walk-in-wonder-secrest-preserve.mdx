_Second of seven hikes in the <a href="/2026-08-29-walk-in-wonder">Walk in Wonder 2026 hike-a-thon</a>._

If there's one word to describe Secrest Preserve, it's BUTTERFLIES. 🦋

The trail is a one-mile loop, with the northern half passing through
oak-hickory forest and the southern half more densely packed with understory
shrubs. We were delighted (and a bit surprised!) to find another patch of
buttonbush, with its pincushion flowers, in a sunny clearing. Buttonbushes are
key nectar sources for bees, hummingbirds, and—as you can see in the
photos—a favorite of butterflies!
